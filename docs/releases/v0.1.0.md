# GazeLift 0.1.0

Give your eyes a little distance. GazeLift lives in the macOS menu bar and reminds
you to look away after 30 minutes of continuous work.

- A quiet green border grows inward over 60 seconds until it fills each display.
- Start a break or snooze for five minutes using the visible reminder buttons.
- Inactivity for 30 seconds, locking, or sleeping automatically ends a work session.
- After a manual break, wait 30 seconds; only fresh input after that wait starts another session.
- English and Simplified Chinese, configurable timing, and optional open at login.

## Install

Download `GazeLift-0.1.0-macos-universal.dmg`, open it, and drag GazeLift to Applications.
The matching ZIP contains the same app. Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple Silicon or Intel.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks an app you trust, attempt
to open it, then use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
See [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

Checksums are published in `SHA256SUMS`. Verify provenance with:

```sh
gh attestation verify GazeLift-0.1.0-macos-universal.dmg --repo laixintao/gazelift
```
